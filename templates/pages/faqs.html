Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our direct China electronics sourcing pipeline, delivery to Rwanda, and warranties.

1. What is the meaning of "BEFORE YOU BUY ELECTRONICS, ASK Z&D"?

In Rwanda, consumers often overpay for electronics or receive refurbished devices disguised as new. By contacting Z&D first, we give you transparent factory pricing directly from vetted electronics suppliers in Shenzhen and Guangzhou, China.

2. Are prices on the website in Rwandan Francs (RWF)?

Yes. All product listings and shipping estimates on our website are explicitly displayed in Rwandan Francs (RWF). There are no hidden foreign exchange conversion surprises.

3. How long does shipping from China take to Rwanda?

For items currently in our Kigali Stock, delivery takes 2 to 24 hours. For Direct China Factory Orders, items are inspected in Shenzhen within 24–48 hours and arrive in Kigali via express air cargo within 7 to 12 business days.

4. How do I pay for my order?

We currently support WhatsApp direct order confirmation, MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, and Cash on Delivery for customers in Kigali. You inspect your device physically before final handover.

5. How does Z&D guarantee the quality of devices?

Every smartphone, laptop, charger, and power bank undergoes our multi-point pre-dispatch inspection at our Shenzhen hub: screen touch matrix verification, battery health check, authentic IMEI validation, and high-wattage charging test.

6. Is my personal customer information private and isolated?

Absolutely. Our system enforces strict database-level and server-side customer isolation. No other customer can ever see your orders, phone numbers, delivery addresses, or personal records.
